SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — NOT REAL RESEARCH

This paragraph is written for the example only. The numbers and the study are invented
so that the example can be shipped without any third-party text.

We introduce a depth-pruned transformer variant called PruneFormer and evaluate it on a
small translation benchmark. PruneFormer reaches 26.8 BLEU on the development split,
which is 1.1 BLEU below the unpruned baseline. The pruning removes 38 percent of
attention heads while keeping the parameter count unchanged.

Training used four GPUs for eighteen hours. We report the median over five seeds and do
not claim statistical significance. Removing the head-pruning step restores the lost BLEU,
so the pruning itself explains the gap rather than the training budget.

We conclude that depth pruning costs 1.1 BLEU in this setting and we do not claim that the
cost transfers to larger benchmarks.
